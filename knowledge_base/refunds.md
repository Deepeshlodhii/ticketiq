# Refund Policy

Customers can request a refund for eligible purchases within 7 days of the transaction.

Refund eligibility depends on the product, purchase date, and account status.

Approved refunds are normally processed within 5-7 business days.

If a refund was approved but has not arrived after 7 business days, the ticket should be reviewed by support.

For refund requests, the support agent may use the check_refund_eligibility tool when an order ID is available.
