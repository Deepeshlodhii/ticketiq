# Billing Support

Customers can view invoices and payment history from the Billing section of their account.

If a customer was charged twice for the same transaction, support should verify the transaction before issuing a refund.

For failed payments where money was deducted, the payment status should be checked before taking action.

Unexpected charges should be verified against the customer's invoice and subscription status.

Billing issues involving duplicate charges, incorrect invoices, or failed payments should be handled using the customer's transaction or order information when available.
